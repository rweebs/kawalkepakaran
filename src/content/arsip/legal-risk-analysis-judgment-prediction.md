## Case Summary

In October 2023, approximately a month after the victim was terminated from AWS Indonesia, Marcello Faria (@sicupuh) and 19 other ITB alumni initiated a massive coordinated cyberattack. The initial viral tweet painted the victim as "a person who ruins the alma mater's reputation," creating a false narrative that sparked a 2.5-year targeted harassment campaign.

## Violated Legal Articles

| No | Source | Article Number | Type of Violation | Threat/Implication |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | UU ITE 1/2024 | Article 27A | Defamation via Electronic Systems | Base electronic defamation offense |
| 2 | UU ITE 1/2024 | Article 45 (4) | Sanction for Article 27A | Max 2 years imprisonment / Rp400M fine |
| 3 | KUHP 1/2023 | Article 433 | Oral/Written Defamation | Max 9 months imprisonment |
| 4 | KUHP 1/2023 | Article 441 | Aggravating Circumstance | 1/3 penalty increase for using IT means |
| 5 | KUH Perdata | Article 1365 | Tort (Unlawful Act) | Basis for material & immaterial civil damages |

### Verified Primary Quotes

> **UU ITE 1/2024 Article 27A:** "Any Person who intentionally attacks the honor or reputation of another person by accusing them of something, with the intention that the matter becomes public knowledge in the form of Electronic Information and/or Electronic Documents transmitted via Electronic Systems."
>
> **KUHP 1/2023 Article 433 (1):** "Any Person who orally attacks the honor or reputation of another person by accusing them of something, with the intention that the matter becomes public knowledge, shall be punished for defamation..."
>
> **KUH Perdata Article 1365:** "Every unlawful act that brings damage to another person obliges the person whose fault caused the damage to compensate for it."

## Impact on Victim and Recovery Options

### Victim's Rights

- Right to demand material damages (loss of income, medical bills) and immaterial damages (reputational harm, trauma).
- Right to demand full restoration of name and reputation.
- Right to demand the immediate takedown of defamatory statements and narratives.

### Scenario Analysis

- **Low-Severity Scenario:** Defendants issue a public apology, remove all traces of the harassment, and pay nominal damages.
- **Moderate-Severity Scenario:** The court imposes suspended sentences on the primary instigators, accompanied by substantial civil damages to cover medical and lost income costs.
- **High-Severity Scenario:** The ringleaders (especially the initiator) face active prison time under the ITE Law, while the collective faces a massive civil judgment in the billions of Rupiah.

### Additional Legal Notes

> Note: Claims under Article 1365 of the Civil Code must be filed within the statute of limitations. The victim should proceed with the summons (Somasi) immediately to halt the ongoing 2.5-year campaign.
