This post sets those public lines beside what the screenshots actually show.

**About Netra Runtime.** His LinkedIn Experience section lists him as Chief Executive Officer of Netra Runtime, a permanent full-time role from May 2026 to present (6 months at capture), in Yogyakarta, Indonesia, hybrid. That entry is the only evidence of Netra Runtime in the screenshots. A web search on October 18, 2026 found no public page for it, so what the company does, and whether it has users or a product, is not verifiable here.

## What the record shows

![Claims against what the screenshots show](/img/arsip-rifky-claims-vs-record.webp)

**The degree.** The LinkedIn entry gives an end year of 2026 and, in its own description field, the single word "dropout". The screenshot does not say when he left, why, or whether a different programme or leave arrangement applies. The entry is his own profile text, and this post takes it as written.

**The scholarship.** The Instagram post shows that he was a BIM batch 1 overseas awardee in 2022. The screenshots do not show the terms of that award, its status in 2026, or any link between the award and the degree entry. This post draws no conclusion about either and does not suggest any breach. Any such question belongs to the scholarship's administrators, not to a screenshot comparison.

**The headline and Netra Runtime.** "Making AI Accessible to Anyone" is a positioning line, and the Netra Runtime entry is the role he gives himself under it. The screenshots show no product, repository, user count or company page behind either, so both are neither confirmed nor refuted. One timing fact is visible on the profile itself: the Netra Runtime role starts in May 2026, inside the 2022 to 2026 range of the UBC entry. The screenshots do not say how the two relate, and the post draws no conclusion from it.

**A public Threads reply.** In a Threads thread captured on October 18, 2026, an account with the handle rifkybujanabisri replied "RW mah emang Musuh bebuyutan 1001 orang WKWKWK", roughly "RW has always been an enemy of 1,001 people, haha". The author, Rahmat Wibowo, reads "RW" as a reference to himself, but the reply does not name anyone, so that reading is not confirmed. The author treats it as an informal remark, not as a statement of fact, and gives it no weight in the assessment above.

## What cuts the other way

- Being chosen as a BIM awardee and admitted to UBC are real, competitive achievements, and the Instagram post shows them in his own words.
- Leaving a degree is a personal decision. It is not a wrongdoing, and many people build useful work without one.
- A company that is six months old can reasonably have no public product page yet, and the role is his own disclosure.
- He wrote "dropout" himself on a public profile rather than hiding it. That is plain disclosure.
- The Threads reply is a joke-style comment, written in a casual register, and does not accuse anyone of anything specific.
- Several things are not verifiable here: what Netra Runtime does, any product or code, its team or customers, his current studies or work, the scholarship's status, and the reason for any change in plans.
