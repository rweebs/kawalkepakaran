*The speaker at a Telkom-branded event on 8 November 2026 promotes research affiliation with MIT. I looked at what the public records show. This article lists what I found, what I did not find, and why neither is a verdict.*

This article is a narrow record. It concerns one speaker's public self-description and the public database entry I captured. It is **not** a finding of fraud, and the evidence below does not prove one.

## What the public database shows

The public higher-education database (PDDikti) entry I captured shows a Bachelor of Informatics student at Universitas Teknologi Yogyakarta, entered 22 September 2020, with last status "Aktif 2025/2026 Ganjil" (active, odd semester 2025/2026).

A third-party video thumbnail I saw on 8 November shows a status of "Non-Aktif 2025/2026 Genap" (inactive, even semester 2025/2026). That is a secondary source, it differs from my own capture, and it may reflect a later date. I have not verified it.

## Three things people mix up

1. **Being a student in the database is not a contradiction.** The database says the speaker is an undergraduate. Many undergraduates also take part in research with outside labs. The listing alone does not disprove the LinkedIn entry.
2. **"Research Student" is not a defined credential.** It does not say enrolled, degree-seeking, visiting, volunteer or collaborator. Whether it is accurate depends on what the relationship with the institution actually is.
3. **I found no proof, and that is not proof of absence.** I have not found an enrolment record, supervisor, publication or output in the speaker's name at MIT. That is only what I found. MIT may hold records I cannot see. The only way to resolve this is a written answer from the institution or from the speaker's own documents.

## What I do not know

- Whether the affiliation exists in some form I have not seen.
- The speaker's current database status.
- Whether Telkom AI Center of Excellence or AI Connect knew of the profile claims when it chose the speaker.
- Whether the company listed on the poster as a collaborator has any formal link to the organisers. I only know what the poster shows.

I also searched the national company registry by name for the speaker's company and saw "Pencarian Tidak Ditemukan" (not found). That search ran from a phone, hit a captcha error first, and searched by name only. It means nothing by itself. A formal request to the registry would settle it.

## Why this matters to an event organiser

The poster, not the speaker's profile, carries the Telkom logos. A logo tells students that someone checked. If it cannot be shown that the check was made, the logo lends credit that nobody has earned yet. That is the reason I asked Telkom to verify from primary sources, and I set out the requests in [what I asked Telkom to do](/arsip/artikel/telkom-integrity-line-complaint-001-aduan-wbs-rw-xi-2026-what-i-asked-telkom-to-do).
