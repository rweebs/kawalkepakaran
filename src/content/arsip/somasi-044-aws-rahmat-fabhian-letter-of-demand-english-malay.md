*Bilingual edition (English and Malay). Somasi No. 044/SOMASI/RW/VIII/2026 is a formal legal notice I served on 31 August 2026. Because one named recipient, Mr. Rahmat Fabhian Aminuddin, works for Amazon Web Services Malaysia in Kuala Lumpur, I also issued a Malay-language version (Surat Tuntutan). The letter speaks of "alleged" corporate crime and defamation, and so do I. It is not a court finding.*

*Edisi dwibahasa (Inggeris dan Melayu). Somasi No. 044/SOMASI/RW/VIII/2026 ialah notis undang-undang rasmi yang saya sampaikan pada 31 Ogos 2026. Oleh sebab seorang penerima, Encik Rahmat Fabhian Aminuddin, bekerja di Amazon Web Services Malaysia di Kuala Lumpur, saya turut mengeluarkan versi Bahasa Melayu. ## English

### Recipients

PT Amazon Web Services Indonesia; Amazon Web Services Malaysia Sdn. Bhd.; AWS ASEAN / Asia Pacific & Japan leadership; Amazon Web Services, Inc. and Amazon.com, Inc.; and three individuals: Ms. Dian Hamama (HR Business Partner, AWS Indonesia), Ms. Veny Charnita Ginting (a former colleague), and Mr. Rahmat Fabhian Aminuddin (Solutions Architect, AWS Malaysia). Carbon copies went to Indonesian and Malaysian police and ministries, KBRI Kuala Lumpur, the South Jakarta Manpower Sub-Agency (case e-0392/KT.03.03), and the Malaysian Immigration Department.

### Summary of the allegations

I was dismissed on 14 September 2023, about 1.5 months into my job at AWS Indonesia and before probation ended, effective 22 September 2023, without signing a termination document or completing documented bipartite negotiation. On 27 September 2023 I emailed HR for help while a thread about me was viral; I state the email was ignored. From October 2023 through 2026, forum accounts using names such as "Amazon AWSome" and "Amazon Bedrock" posted degrading and intimidating statements about me. I have not established who operates them. I reported Mr. Aminuddin to the police (LP/B/2914/VII/2026/SPKT/South Jakarta Metro Police, 23 July 2026) over a post dated 17 March 2026. I also acknowledge in the letter that in a difficult period in 2023 I used an offensive word that I regret.

On 14 July 2026, counsel for AWS Indonesia asked me to withdraw all claims before any institution within 14 days. I read that as obstructing my access to official forums; AWS Indonesia may see it differently.

### Demands (deadline: Thursday, 3 September 2026, 17:00 WIB)

1. Remove content attacking me, within each party's control;
2. AWS Indonesia to issue a written position statement, withdraw point 12 of its 14 July letter, and confirm no retaliation for my use of lawful processes;
3. Written apologies from AWS Indonesia and the three individuals;
4. Written clarifications from the three individuals;
5. A responsible officer and formal coordination channel, with escalation to AWS regional and global levels;
6. Non-material compensation, plus a sum for AWS Indonesia's corporate responsibility (minimums), and a separate minimum from Mr. Aminuddin.

If the demands are not met, I stated I would file criminal reports (including a corporate liability report), a civil suit, escalated complaints, and, as to Mr. Aminuddin, a complaint in Malaysia to the MCMC and Royal Malaysia Police and a request to the Malaysian Immigration Department to review his Employment Pass.

## Bahasa Melayu

### Penerima

PT Amazon Web Services Indonesia; Amazon Web Services Malaysia Sdn. Bhd.; kepimpinan AWS ASEAN / Asia Pacific & Japan; Amazon Web Services, Inc. dan Amazon.com, Inc.; serta tiga individu: Puan Dian Hamama (Rakan Kongsi Perniagaan Sumber Manusia Strategik, AWS Indonesia), Puan Veny Charnita Ginting (bekas rakan sekerja), dan Encik Rahmat Fabhian Aminuddin (Solutions Architect, AWS Malaysia). Salinan dihantar kepada polis dan kementerian Indonesia dan Malaysia, KBRI Kuala Lumpur, Sub-Badan Tenaga Kerja Jakarta Selatan (perkara e-0392/KT.03.03), dan Jabatan Imigresen Malaysia.

### Ringkasan dakwaan

Saya diberhentikan pada 14 September 2023, kira-kira 1.5 bulan selepas bekerja di AWS Indonesia dan sebelum tempoh percubaan tamat, berkuat kuasa 22 September 2023, tanpa menandatangani dokumen penamatan dan tanpa rundingan bipartit yang didokumenkan. Pada 27 September 2023 saya menghantar e-mel kepada HR memohon bantuan ketika satu utas mengenai saya tular; saya menyatakan e-mel itu diabaikan. Dari Oktober 2023 hingga 2026, akaun forum yang menggunakan nama seperti "Amazon AWSome" dan "Amazon Bedrock" menyiarkan kenyataan yang menghina dan mengugut saya. Saya belum dapat memastikan siapa pengendali akaun tersebut. Saya melaporkan Encik Aminuddin kepada polis (LP/B/2914/VII/2026/SPKT/Polres Metro Jakarta Selatan, 23 Julai 2026) atas hantaran bertarikh 17 Mac 2026. Dalam surat itu saya juga mengakui bahawa pada fasa mania tahun 2023 saya pernah menggunakan perkataan yang menyinggung dan saya kesali.

Pada 14 Julai 2026, peguam AWS Indonesia meminta saya menarik balik semua tuntutan kepada mana-mana institusi dalam tempoh 14 hari. Saya menganggap ini sebagai menghalang akses saya kepada forum rasmi; AWS Indonesia mungkin berpandangan lain.

### Tuntutan (tempoh: Khamis, 3 September 2026, pukul 17.00 WIB)

1. Membuang kandungan yang menyerang saya, setakat dalam kawalan masing-masing;
2. AWS Indonesia mengeluarkan penyataan pendirian bertulis, menarik balik perenggan 12 surat 14 Julai, dan mengesahkan tiada tindakan balas atas penggunaan saluran undang-undang yang sah;
3. Permohonan maaf bertulis daripada AWS Indonesia dan tiga individu;
4. Penjelasan bertulis daripada tiga individu;
5. Pegawai bertanggungjawab dan saluran penyelarasan rasmi, dengan peningkatan isu ke peringkat serantau dan global AWS;
6. Ganti rugi bukan material serta satu jumlah sebagai tanggungjawab korporat AWS Indonesia (jumlah minimum), dan jumlah minimum yang berasingan daripada Encik Aminuddin.

Jika tuntutan tidak dipenuhi, saya menyatakan akan memfailkan laporan jenayah (termasuk laporan tanggungjawab jenayah korporat), tuntutan sivil, aduan berperingkat, dan, terhadap Encik Aminuddin, aduan di Malaysia kepada MCMC dan Polis Diraja Malaysia serta permohonan kepada Jabatan Imigresen Malaysia untuk menyemak Pas Pekerjaannya.
