A mentor's homepage is the promise a prospective mentee reads first. This post reviews gedearya.com, the career mentoring site of I Gede Arya Raditya Parameswara, on what is publicly visible: the search and accessibility basics are strong, one mobile speed figure is very weak, and the headline claim is displayed with logos but no numbers.

*This is my opinion and analysis of publicly visible material. The PageSpeed figures are from screenshots dated 21 June 2026, not from a fresh run, and the rest of the review was done on 27 September 2026 from screenshots. I did not test rankings, traffic or mentoring outcomes, and I am not judging the person. I Gede Arya Raditya Parameswara is invited to respond (see the end of this post).*

## What holds up

- **The technical foundation is good.** SEO is 100 on both desktop and mobile, Best Practices is 96 on both, and Accessibility is 92 on desktop and 96 on mobile. CLS is 0 on both, so nothing jumps while the page loads.
- **The desktop paint times are fast.** First Contentful Paint is 0.2 s and Largest Contentful Paint is 0.8 s.
- **Mobile blocking time is low.** Total Blocking Time is 20 ms on mobile.
- **The pitch is clear.** "Early career mentor and builder" tells a visitor who the service is for.
- **The engineering background is visible.** His LinkedIn entry lists a Software Engineer role at Xendit (full-time, August 2022 to February 2024, Jakarta, remote), described as streamlining the merchant onboarding flow and improving verification speed and security, built with TypeScript on fintech infrastructure.

## What does not hold up

**1. Mobile Largest Contentful Paint is 17.7 seconds.** Everything else on the mobile report is good, which makes this figure stand out. FCP is 1.5 s and TBT is 20 ms, yet the largest element takes 17.7 s to appear. Google treats 2.5 s or less as good. On a mentoring site whose visitors are mostly on phones, this is the one fix that would matter most.

**2. Desktop Total Blocking Time is 1,390 ms.** Desktop LCP is fine at 0.8 s, but script work blocks the page for well over a second, which is why desktop Performance is 67. Both this and the mobile LCP can be fixed without changing the design.

**3. The headline claim has no numbers.** "I've helped mentees land their dream jobs" is a specific promise. The page shows logos but, as far as the screenshots show, no count of mentees, no named outcomes and no time frame, and it does not say whether each logo means an employee, a mentee placement or something else. A prospective mentee cannot tell what the grid measures. A single line such as "12 mentees hired since 2024, at the companies below" would answer it, and would also address the logo question directly.

**4. The logo grid raises a question the page does not answer.** Displaying the logo of a company where a mentee was hired is a common way to describe a result, and it is not obviously a violation. The open question is evidence: whether each logo reflects a real placement, and whether the page says so. I am not a court and I make no legal finding. The complaint page reproduced below lists Indonesian and UK statutes and sanctions. That table is the complainant's position and not a ruling, and I do not treat it as a conclusion.

## What a prospective mentee should ask for

1. **The number and the definition:** how many mentees, over what period, and what "landed a job" means (offer, start date, role).
2. **Named or permissioned examples:** testimonials from mentees who agree to be named, matched to the logos shown.
3. **What each logo stands for:** a line under the grid that says whether it shows employers of mentees, his own employers or something else.
4. **A mobile speed fix:** ask whether the 17.7 s mobile LCP has been addressed, since a mentee will open the site on a phone.
5. **Written terms:** price, session count and what is refunded if there is no result.

## Limits of this review

- I did not test rankings, traffic or the accuracy of any claim about mentoring results.

- I am not a court. Nothing here is a legal finding on trademarks, contracts or employment terms.
- Strong SEO, Best Practices and Accessibility scores are real. They do not measure whether the mentoring works.
- This is my opinion and not professional certification of anyone's competence.
