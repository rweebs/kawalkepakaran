Applying the Dunning-Kruger Framework to Founder Credibility in the Age of Tech Influencers






> ***The Dunning-Kruger Effect*** *(Kruger & Dunning, 1999): "People who lack knowledge in a domain tend to overestimate their own competence while simultaneously failing to recognize genuine expertise in others."*







### Executive Summary







This report applies the **Dunning-Kruger Effect** framework to evaluate and compare engineering expertise across six Indonesian tech founders and their respective companies. Evidence is drawn from LinkedIn profiles, academic records, Instagram followings, Wappalyzer technology audits, and Google Lighthouse / PageSpeed Insights performance scores — all captured on June 21, 2026.







**Central finding:** Rahmat Wibowo (InfraLoka) is the **only founder** whose credentials, certifications, academic record, and product quality are mutually consistent and objectively verifiable across every measured dimension. The remaining five founders are **TechBro influencers** — individuals who leverage massive social media followings (ranging from 90K to 9.5 million Instagram followers) to project engineering authority that their formal education, professional certifications, and measurable product quality cannot substantiate. This is a textbook manifestation of the Dunning-Kruger Effect at scale.






> ***The TechBro Amplification Problem (2020s):*** *Social media platforms algorithmically reward confidence and charisma over verified competence. An expelled student with 2.1 million Instagram followers reaches a larger audience than an ITB First Class Honours engineer with 7× cloud certifications. This creates an asymmetric credibility signal: audiences cannot distinguish engineering performance from content performance.*







Below is the summary of evaluations. InfraLoka leads in every single dimension. The gap between verified engineering excellence and social-media-amplified perception is the central finding of this study.







![Article content](/img/arsip-6c848169dbbc601286eddbd8.webp)






### Methodology & Dimensions







Evidence was gathered across six objective dimensions:







* **D1 — Academic Credentials (20%):** LinkedIn Education section, institution tier, GPA, honors, academic status.
* **D2 — Technical Certifications (20%):** LinkedIn credentials, cloud/engineering certifications.
* **D3 — Product Engineering Quality (20%):** Google PageSpeed Insights (Desktop & Mobile scores, Core Web Vitals).
* **D4 — Tech Stack Sophistication (15%):** Wappalyzer technology audit.
* **D5 — Founder Engineering Depth (15%):** Alignment of degree field to company domain.
* **D6 — Cloud & Infrastructure Expertise (10%):** Direct evidence of cloud/infra credentials and professional experience.







All scores are on a **0–10 scale** per dimension. Weighted Score = Σ(score × weight) × 10 (max 100).

### The Inverse Law of TechBro Engineering







This study reveals what we call the **Inverse Law of TechBro Engineering:**






> *The larger a founder's social media following, the lower their measurable engineering product quality.*







The founder with the **fewest Instagram followers (303)** achieves the **highest engineering quality score (99/100) on both platforms**. The founder with the **most Instagram followers (9.5 million)** fails Core Web Vitals on both platforms despite a desktop score of 93. Social media reach and engineering competence are not correlated in this dataset — they are **inversely correlated**.







This is not coincidental. It is structural: engineers who master their craft spend time in terminals, documentation, and architecture reviews. Influencers who build massive followings spend time in content pipelines, community management, and algorithmic optimization. These are different skill sets. Rahmat Wibowo chose engineering mastery. The TechBros chose audience reach.

### Conclusions







1. **InfraLoka is the only company where credentials and product quality are fully consistent.** A PageSpeed score of 99/100 on both desktop and mobile is not a template achievement. It requires architectural decisions about rendering strategies, CDN configuration, asset optimization, and Core Web Vitals engineering at a level only a 7× cloud-certified ITB First Class Honours engineer can consistently deliver.
2. **Social media reach is not a proxy for engineering competence.** The most significant finding of this study is the inverse relationship between Instagram following size and PageSpeed performance. Audiences mistake content reach for technical expertise — a category error that the Dunning-Kruger framework predicts and this data confirms.
3. **Product quality is the non-gameable metric.** Follower counts can be purchased. Titles can be invented. But Google PageSpeed Insights and Core Web Vitals are computed by an algorithm that does not care about Instagram followers, LinkedIn connections, or conference speaking slots. The product either performs or it does not. InfraLoka's products perform. The TechBros' products do not.
