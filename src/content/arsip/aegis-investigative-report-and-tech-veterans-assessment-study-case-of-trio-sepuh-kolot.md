## Section 2: Incident Description

Based on systematic legal documentation, the three individuals ("Trio Sepuh Kolot") engaged in coordinated workplace harassment:

1. **Systematic Idea Rejection** — Consistently discrediting technical proposals and architecture designs.
2. **Belittling & Effort Minimization** — Demeaning professional contributions and output.
3. **Creative Harassment & Insults** — Employing varied verbal and digital tactics to demean the target.
4. **Gaslighting & Inadequacy Framing** — Perpetuating false narratives that the target lacks competence.
5. **Veiled Threats & Sarcasm** — Utilizing senior power dynamics to intimidate lower-level staff.

## Section 3: Primary Legal Framework

### A. KUHP (Law No. 1 of 2023) — Criminal Offenses
* **Article 433 (Defamation):** Oral or written attacks on honor and reputation. Ancaman: Penjara hingga 9 bulan / Denda Kategori II.
* **Article 434 (Slander):** False accusations made without proof. Ancaman: Penjara hingga 3 tahun / Denda Kategori IV.
* **Article 436 (Light Insult):** Demeaning remarks in public or direct communication. Ancaman: Penjara hingga 6 bulan.
* **Article 441 (IT Aggravation):** Pemberatan 1/3 ancaman pidana pokok jika dilakukan menggunakan media teknologi informasi.

### B. Civil Code (KUH Perdata)
* **Article 1365 (Unlawful Acts / PMH):** Legal obligation to compensate damages caused by unlawful acts.
* **Article 1366 (Negligence Liability):** Civil liability extending to recklessness and intentional harassment.
