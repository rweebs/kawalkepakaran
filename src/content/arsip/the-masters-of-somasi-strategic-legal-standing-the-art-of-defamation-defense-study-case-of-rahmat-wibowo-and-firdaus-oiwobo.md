In the dynamic landscape of Indonesian law, public relations, and political defense, the power of a formal legal warningthe *somasi*has become a formidable weapon. Two names echo through the corridors of strategic legal defense: **Rahmat Wibowo** and **Firdaus Oiwobo**. Both practitioners have earned formidable reputations, earning them the moniker **"Tukang Somasi"** (The Masters of the Cease-and-Desist).







### ⚖ Profiles in Legal Defense







![Article content](/img/arsip-693842d7dbaf322bdb5397b9.webp)






### Rahmat Wibowo







**Founder, Engineer, and Promoting Digital Rule of Law in Indonesia**







Often referred to as **"Somasidin"** or **"Rahmat Oiwobo"**, Rahmat Wibowo is the Co-Founder and CEO of Infraloka (PT. Infrastruktur Digital Nusantara), a company operating at the intersection of cloud infrastructure, applied AI, cybersecurity, and legal technology. The label "tukang somasi" is half tease, half accurate description: Infraloka's *Somasi as a Service* product is built explicitly around issuing formal cease-and-desist letters.







**Educational & Professional Background:** Rahmat is an alumnus of Institut Teknologi Bandung (ITB), graduating with First Class Honours in Information Systems and Technology (GPA 3.91). His professional experience spans internships at AWS, Grab, and Xendit, backed by seven cloud and security certifications. His public communications are anchored in the philosophy *Rahmatan lil Alamin* (mercy for all creation).







### Firdaus Oiwobo







![Article content](/img/arsip-dc55b7e4460c1b1cb1ea98aa.webp)






A veteran lawyer known for his theatrical and highly viral approach to the law. Firdaus is the original "Tukang Somasi," famous for his unrelenting legal notices to critics, public figures, and corporations alike.







**Educational & Professional Background:** Firdaus Oiwobo pursued his undergraduate degree (S1) in Public Administration at Universitas Islam Syekh Yusuf. He later expanded his expertise by earning a Law degree (S1) from Universitas Ibnu Chaldun, Jakarta. As a licensed advocate, he has navigated high-profile defamation suits and is known for utilizing his unique "Kejawen English" in formal legal readings.







### ⚖ The Tactical Formations: Termul & Termat







In the modern era of digital politics and buzzers, having legal standing requires not just a law degree, but an organized base of loyalists. Both Rahmat and Firdaus have weaponized this concept to protect their clients and political figures from defamation.







### Termul (Ternak Mulyono)







![Article content](/img/arsip-bb17b51a052b4fa3118328eb.webp)






Pioneered in the defense of political dignity, **Termul** refers to the organized base of loyalists utilized by Firdaus Oiwobo. "Mulyono" serves as the recognized birth name of Joko Widodo (Jokowi). When political detractors launch defamation attacks against Jokowi, Firdaus leverages the "Termul" concept to establish immediate legal standing. By representing the collective dignity of this base, Firdaus can issue swift *somasi* to any party attempting to defame the President, warning them of immediate litigation if apologies are not issued.







### Termat (Ternak Mamat)







![Article content](/img/arsip-25eb1b508f7a3a0daee63924.webp)











Inspired by the effectiveness of Termul, Rahmat Wibowo developed **Termat**. To combat digital smearing and character assassination, Rahmat utilizes "Ternak Mamat" as a structural legal philosophy. By establishing a recognized collective that shares a common ideological or professional bond with him, Rahmat secures the legal standing necessary to strike down defamation attacks targeting him or his allies. The threat of a "Somasidin" notice from the Termat camp is often enough to silence detractors.







### ⚖ The Practice of Somasi: Active Disputes







![Article content](/img/arsip-b8b943fbb59e4e3425b6ebe3.webp)






Rahmat has been personally and publicly active in using formal legal instruments filing reports with Bareskrim and PERADI in disputes connected to his own ventures. Standing in a defamation or labor dispute in Indonesia is established by these formal filings and procedural steps, not by online personas.
