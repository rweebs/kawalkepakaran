## What the logical audit finds / 逻辑审查结论 / Luójí shěnchá jiélùn

**中文（汉字）**

核心问题不是哪一方说得更响，而是哪一项结论有可检验的证据。把某人称为“疯子”不能证明其法律主张错误，这是人身攻击。声称某人假装精神异常以逃避责任，却没有提供可核实证据，属于动机揣测和毒化论域。把使用人工智能工具当作无能力或欺诈的证明，是不相干结论。用嘲笑、监狱、学历或工作经历代替对原始指控的证明，是诉诸嘲讽或关联谬误。

同样，拉赫马特账号的部分回复也用“愚蠢”“不正常”“大脑像虾”等人身攻击，并用学历、作品集或付费工具来概括能力。这些回复不能反驳对方的事实指控，反而复制了同一种错误。因此，这份记录展示的不是一个严谨的辩论，而是双方不断升级的人身化争执。

**Hànyǔ Pīnyīn**

Héxīn wèntí bú shì nǎ yìfāng shuō de gèng xiǎng, ér shì nǎ yí xiàng jiélùn yǒu kě jiǎnyàn de zhèngjù. Bǎ mǒurén chēngwéi “fēngzi” bù néng zhèngmíng qí fǎlǜ zhǔzhāng cuòwù, zhè shì rénshēn gōngjī. Shēngchēng mǒurén jiǎzhuāng jīngshén yìcháng yǐ táobì zérèn, què méiyǒu tígōng kě héshí zhèngjù, shǔyú dòngjī chuǎicè hé dúhuà lùnyù. Bǎ shǐyòng réngōng zhìnéng gōngjù dàngzuò wú nénglì huò qīzhà de zhèngmíng, shì bù xiānggān jiélùn. Yòng cháoxiào, jiānyù, xuélì huò gōngzuò jīnglì dàitì duì yuánshǐ zhǐkòng de zhèngmíng, shì sùzhū cháofěng huò guānlián miùwù.

Tóngyàng, Lāhèmǎtè zhànghào de bùfèn huífù yě yòng “yúchǔn”, “bù zhèngcháng”, “dànǎo xiàng xiā” děng rénshēn gōngjī, bìng yòng xuélì, zuòpǐnjí huò fùfèi gōngjù lái gàikuò nénglì. Zhèxiē huífù bù néng fǎnbó duìfāng de shìshí zhǐkòng, fǎn'ér fùzhìle tóng yì zhǒng cuòwù. Yīncǐ, zhè fèn jìlù zhǎnshì de bú shì yí gè yánjǐn de biànlùn, ér shì shuāngfāng bùduàn shēngjí de rénshēnhuà zhēngzhí.

**English translation**

The central question is not who speaks more loudly, but which conclusion has testable evidence. Calling someone “insane” cannot establish that his legal claim is wrong: that is ad hominem. Claiming that someone pretends to be mentally unwell to avoid responsibility, without verifiable evidence, is motive speculation and poisoning the well. Treating the use of AI tools as proof of incompetence or fraud is a non sequitur. Substituting ridicule, prison references, education, or employment history for proof of the original accusation is appeal to ridicule or guilt by association.

Some replies from Rahmat's account likewise use personal attacks such as “stupid,” “not sane,” and “shrimp brain,” while treating degrees, portfolios, or paid tools as general measures of competence. Those replies do not rebut the factual accusations; they reproduce the same error. The record therefore shows escalating personalized conflict, not a disciplined debate.

| Pattern | What appears in the screenshots | Why it fails |
|---|---|---|
| Ad hominem / 人身攻击 / rénshēn gōngjī | “orgil,” “mentally disturbed,” “kebodohan,” “otak udang” | A personal label does not resolve the disputed facts. |
| Unsupported criminal accusation / 无证据的犯罪指控 / wú zhèngjù de fànzuì zhǐkòng | “tried to extort,” “scammer” | A serious conclusion needs primary evidence and defined elements. |
| Appeal to ridicule / 诉诸嘲讽 / sùzhū cháofěng | “Kuntiverse,” “Teletubbies,” “planet AWS,” laughter | Mockery supplies emotion, not proof. |
| Non sequitur / 不相干结论 / bù xiānggān jiélùn | “uses ChatGPT” therefore incompetent, fake, or fraudulent | Tool choice does not establish the quality or truth of the output. |
| Hasty generalization / 草率概括 / cǎoshuài gàikuò | One screenshot, role, or reply used to judge total ability or character | The sample is too narrow for the conclusion. |
| Mind-reading / 动机揣测 / dòngjī chuǎicè | Claims that a person is acting ill, afraid, or out of ideas | Internal motives require evidence, not inference from tone. |
| Tu quoque / 反唇相讥 / fǎnchún xiāngjī | Each side answers insult with insult | Showing an opponent's bad conduct does not prove one's own claim. |
| Appeal to credentials / 诉诸资历 / sùzhū zīlì | Degrees, employers, or job titles used as decisive proof | Credentials may add context but cannot replace evidence. |
| Burden shifting / 转移举证责任 / zhuǎnyí jǔzhèng zérèn | Accusations followed by demands that the target disprove them | The person making the claim carries the initial burden of support. |

## What the record can and cannot prove

**It can support these narrow observations:** the supplied captures display repeated allegations and insults; the same central text appears in several reply contexts; both visible accounts use personal attacks; and the legal notice demands a response and evidence.

No logical-fallacy label answers those factual and legal questions. They require original platform records, complete conversations, authenticated files, witnesses, and fair adjudication.

## A better form of argument / 更好的论证方式 / Gèng hǎo de lùnzhèng fāngshì

**中文（汉字）**

更可靠的讨论应当逐项提出明确主张，提供原始记录、日期、完整上下文和可核实来源，并允许对方回应。不要以精神健康标签、学历、雇主、人工智能工具或嘲笑来代替证据。提出犯罪指控的人首先负有举证责任；被指控者的愤怒或粗鲁回应，也不会自动证明原始指控为真。

**Hànyǔ Pīnyīn**

Gèng kěkào de tǎolùn yīngdāng zhúxiàng tíchū míngquè zhǔzhāng, tígōng yuánshǐ jìlù, rìqī, wánzhěng shàngxiàwén hé kě héshí láiyuán, bìng yǔnxǔ duìfāng huíyìng. Bú yào yǐ jīngshén jiànkāng biāoqiān, xuélì, gùzhǔ, réngōng zhìnéng gōngjù huò cháoxiào lái dàitì zhèngjù. Tíchū fànzuì zhǐkòng de rén shǒuxiān fùyǒu jǔzhèng zérèn; bèi zhǐkòngzhě de fènnù huò cūlǔ huíyìng, yě bú huì zìdòng zhèngmíng yuánshǐ zhǐkòng wéi zhēn.

**English translation**

A reliable discussion should state each claim precisely, supply primary records, dates, complete context, and verifiable sources, and allow the other party to answer. The person making a criminal accusation carries the initial burden of support; an angry or rude response from the accused does not automatically make the original accusation true.
