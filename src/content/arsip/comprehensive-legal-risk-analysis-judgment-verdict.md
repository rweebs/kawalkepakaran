## Executive Summary

This report analyzes one of the most severe documented institutional defamation and coordinated cyberbullying cases involving a major multinational technology company and a leading Indonesian university. The victim, Rahmat Wibowo, suffered cascading harm resulting from three overlapping incidents:

**1. Wrongful Termination (Indonesia — AWS, September 2023):** Rahmat was dismissed by PT Amazon Web Services Indonesia during his probation period (1.5 months into employment) while traveling in Singapore on a self-funded tourist visit. The termination — carried out by HR Manager Dian Hamama via written notice dated September 14, 2023 — cited a Medium article he published mentioning his job title "Associate Solutions Architect @ AWS." This was a trivial disclosure that could have been resolved with a simple edit. No prior warning, no formal inquiry, and no opportunity to respond was given before termination. Immediately upon termination, all system access and data were deleted (garden leave protocol), eliminating his ability to retain evidence.

**2. Coordinated Cyberbullying Campaign (Indonesia/Singapore/Malaysia — AWS Accounts + ITB Community, September–October 2023):** Within approximately one month of termination, a coordinated harassment campaign emerged across Twitter/X and the forum platform f/Publik. This campaign involved: (a) accounts displaying Amazon/AWS branding actively participating in public ridicule; (b) accounts badged with ITB's university logo amplifying harassment; (c) a viral thread that reached approximately 2.6 million views (documented in Rahmat's own message to Dian Hamama).

**3. Institutional Defamation by ITB (Indonesia — ongoing):** Confirmed by an active ITB student, Rahmat's name was embedded in the STI (Information Systems and Technology) Professional Ethics curriculum as a "bad attitude" example, spanning approximately 3 chapters, distributed to hundreds of students per cohort, repeated across multiple academic years without his knowledge or consent.

Recovery required approximately 2 years.

**Primary legal strategy:** Indonesia is the natural and most strategic forum. All defendants are either Indonesian entities or Indonesian nationals. All effects of the harmful conduct were experienced in Indonesia. Singapore POHA and Malaysia CMA provide supplementary protection for the cyberbullying experienced during the victim's presence in those countries. **The Singapore Employment Act does not apply** — the victim entered Singapore on a 30-day visa-free tourist entry, not a work pass; the employment contract was with an Indonesian entity.

#### Pasal 433 — Pencemaran Nama Baik (Defamation)

> **Pasal 433 ayat (1):** "Setiap Orang yang dengan lisan menyerang kehormatan atau nama baik orang lain dengan cara menuduhkan suatu hal, dengan maksud supaya hal tersebut diketahui umum, dipidana karena pencemaran, dengan pidana penjara paling lama **9 (sembilan) Bulan** atau pidana denda paling banyak **kategori II**."

> **Pasal 433 ayat (2):** "Jika perbuatan sebagaimana dimaksud pada ayat (1) dilakukan dengan tulisan atau gambar yang disiarkan, dipertunjukkan, atau ditempelkan di tempat umum, dipidana karena pencemaran tertulis, dengan pidana penjara paling lama **1 (satu) tahun 6 (enam) Bulan** atau pidana denda paling banyak **kategori III**."

#### Pasal 434 — Fitnah (Slander / Aggravated Defamation)

> **Pasal 434 ayat (1):** "Jika Setiap Orang sebagaimana dimaksud dalam Pasal 433 diberi kesempatan membuktikan kebenaran hal yang dituduhkan tetapi tidak dapat membuktikannya, dan tuduhan tersebut bertentangan dengan yang diketahuinya, dipidana karena fitnah, dengan pidana penjara paling lama **3 (tiga) tahun** atau pidana denda paling banyak **kategori IV**."

**Application:** The characterization of Rahmat as having "bad attitude" lacking ethics (in ITB's curriculum) and as a "bad example for humanity" (in forum posts) are assertions of fact. When defendants are challenged to prove these claims and cannot, Pasal 434 applies. Maximum: **3 years imprisonment.**

#### Pasal 436 — Penghinaan Ringan (Light Insult)

*"Penghinaan yang tidak bersifat pencemaran atau pencemaran tertulis yang dilakukan terhadap orang lain baik Di Muka Umum dengan lisan atau tulisan, maupun di muka orang yang dihina tersebut secara lisan atau dengan perbuatan atau dengan tulisan yang dikirimkan atau diterimakan kepadanya, dipidana karena penghinaan ringan..."*

Maximum: **6 months** or denda kategori II.

#### Pasal 441 — Pemberatan dengan Teknologi (Technology Aggravation)

> **Pasal 441 ayat (1):** "Ketentuan pidana sebagaimana dimaksud dalam Pasal 433 sampai dengan Pasal 439 dapat ditambah **1/3 (satu per tiga)** jika dilakukan dengan sarana teknologi informasi."

**Application:** All forum posts, Twitter/X posts, private DMs, and messaging app communications are conducted via information technology. ALL penalties above are automatically increased by 1/3. Therefore:
- Pasal 433(2) with Pasal 441: **up to 24 months** (18 months × 4/3)
- Pasal 434 with Pasal 441: **up to 4 years** (3 years × 4/3)

#### Pasal 335 — Criminal Coercion / Intimidation

*Perbuatan tidak menyenangkan / ancaman kekerasan terhadap seseorang.*

**Application:** The private DM threat ("I have that power to force and find you") combined with threats to use legal/police power to harass constitutes criminal coercion under Pasal 335. Maximum: **1 year** imprisonment.

#### Pasal 440 — Delik Aduan (Complaint-Based Prosecution)

> "Tindak Pidana sebagaimana dimaksud dalam Pasal 433, Pasal 434, dan Pasal 436 sampai dengan Pasal 438 tidak dituntut, jika tidak ada pengaduan dari Korban Tindak Pidana."

**Critical procedural note:** Prosecution under Pasal 433, 434, and 436 REQUIRES a formal complaint (laporan/pengaduan) from the victim. This complaint should be filed at Bareskrim Polri or the nearest Polres.

All texts verified from `references/KUH_Perdata.pdf`.

#### Pasal 1365 — Perbuatan Melawan Hukum (PMH)

> "Tiap perbuatan yang melanggar hukum dan membawa kerugian kepada orang lain, mewajibkan orang yang menimbulkan kerugian itu karena kesalahannya untuk menggantikan kerugian tersebut."

**The four PMH elements satisfied:**

| Element | Application |
|---|---|
| Unlawful act | Defamation, harassment, unauthorized curriculum use, wrongful termination |
| Fault | Deliberate — each defendant made a conscious choice to post/teach/terminate |
| Causation | Direct causal chain: termination → viral harassment → career collapse → a health condition → 1.5 years isolation |

#### Pasal 1366 — Liability for Negligence

*"Setiap orang bertanggung jawab, bukan hanya atas kerugian yang disebabkan perbuatannya, melainkan juga atas kerugian yang disebabkan kelalaian atau kesembronoannya."*

**Application:** Even if any defendant argues they did not intend harm, their negligent failure to foresee the consequences makes them liable.

#### Pasal 1367 — Vicarious Liability (Employer/Institution)

> "...Majikan dan orang yang mengangkat orang lain untuk mewakili urusan-urusan mereka, bertanggung jawab atas kerugian yang disebabkan oleh pelayan atau bawahan mereka dalam melakukan pekerjaan yang ditugaskan kepada orang-orang itu."

**Application:** PT Amazon Web Services Indonesia is vicariously liable for the conduct of AWS-branded accounts (AWsome, mkUldh, Bedrock, WhySoSerious, Def8W8). **The use of corporate-branded accounts posting defamatory content makes this vicarious liability very strong** — these were not anonymous accounts; they displayed Amazon's orange logo, creating apparent authority.

Similarly, ITB is vicariously liable for its employee (Basim, identified as an ITB employee) participating in the harassment (E-06).

#### Pasal 1372 — Civil Defamation Claim for Damages + Reputation Restoration

> "Tuntutan perdata tentang hal penghinaan diajukan untuk memperoleh penggantian kerugian serta pemulihan kehormatan dan nama baik. Dalam menilai satu sama lain, hakim harus memperhatikan kasar atau tidaknya penghinaan, begitu pula **pangkat, kedudukan dan kemampuan kedua belah pihak** dan keadaan."

**The "kemampuan" provision is strategically critical:** The judge must calibrate the ganti rugi to the financial capacity (kemampuan) of both parties. Against Amazon (global market cap ~USD 2 trillion) and ITB (a state university with substantial assets), this provision allows Indonesian judges to award significantly higher damages than against an individual defendant. The law explicitly directs this calibration.

#### Pasal 1373 — Declaratory and Remediation Relief

*"Selain itu, orang yang dihina dapat menuntut pula supaya dalam putusan juga dinyatakan bahwa perbuatan yang telah dilakukan adalah perbuatan memfitnah."*

**Application:** The judgment can include a declaration that ITB's curriculum use and the forum posts constitute defamation (fitnah), and can order publication of the verdict at defendants' expense.
