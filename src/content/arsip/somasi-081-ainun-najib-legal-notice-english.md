## Forwarding is a deliberate act of publication

The "Forwarded" label shows that the words were first written elsewhere, and I cannot tell from a screenshot who wrote them. That does not make the forwarding passive. To send those words to about 100 people he had to choose to copy them and choose to post them under his own name, three times in a row. I treat that as his own act of publication: he adopted the allegation as his message, and he stood behind it when he added, in his own words, *"yes, a friend is already upset."* I do not claim he coined the text. I claim he published it knowingly, without checking, and that he answers for that.

## My position

I never intended to threaten to kill anyone, and I was not summoned by the police on the Monday referred to in the text. I know of no such police report. The letter explains that I made a video in response to a Threads thread about me that had about 437,000 views, as a defence and to get that thread taken down. It accepts that others may read the video differently and leaves its legal assessment to the authorities. The letter does not ask him to stop truthful reporting. It asks for a police report number, summons date or other evidence if he has any. My only direct contact with him was a courteous LinkedIn message proposing collaboration.
