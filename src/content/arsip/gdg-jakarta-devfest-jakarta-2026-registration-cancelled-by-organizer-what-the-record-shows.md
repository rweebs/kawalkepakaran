*On 24 October 2026 GDG Jakarta ID emailed me that my DevFest Jakarta 2026 registration had been cancelled by the organizer. The email gave no reason. This article sets out what the emails and the invoice show, and what they do not.*

This is my account as a registrant. It is not a finding by anyone, and it does not accuse any named person of causing the cancellation.

## Scope and identity note

The sources are four screenshots from my own mailbox: the cancellation notice, the email header, the sent time, and a DOKU invoice for the same registration. I removed my phone number, email address and invoice number from the images I publish. The screenshots are not forensically authenticated. I do not know who at the organizer took the decision, or whether a person or a system took it.

## What the emails show

1. **Invoice (24 October 2026).** A DOKU invoice issued for "DevFest Jakarta 2026", with an Early Bird ticket at IDR 125,000, a DevFest T-shirt at IDR 250,000 and a QRIS payment fee of IDR 2,625. Total IDR 377,625. The invoice shows **Amount Paid: 0** and **Amount Due: IDR 377,625**, with a due date of 25 October 2026.
2. **Cancellation notice (24 October 2026, 10:41 PM).** From GDG Jakarta ID, subject "Registration Cancelled: DevFest Jakarta 2026". The text reads: "Your registration has been cancelled by the organizer and you have been removed from the event. If you change your mind, you need to re-register again."

## What this does and does not show

**Documented:** an unpaid invoice due on 25 October; a cancellation email sent on 24 October, one day before that due date, saying the organizer cancelled it.

**Not documented:**

- Why the registration was cancelled. The email gives no reason.
- Whether the cancellation was automatic (for example, a rule that cancels unpaid orders) or a manual decision.
- Whether payment was possible and expected before the due date. I do not know why I had not paid when the notice arrived.
- Who at GDG Jakarta, if anyone, decided.

## Where I may be wrong

- The most ordinary explanation is an automatic or capacity-based cancellation. Events commonly cancel unpaid or surplus registrations, and a system can send "cancelled by the organizer" for that.
- The invoice was unpaid. Any organizer is entitled to release a place that has not been paid for, though cancelling before the stated due date is something I would expect to be explained.
- I have not yet received a written answer, and one may exist that I have not seen.

1. The specific reason my registration was cancelled.
2. Whether unpaid registrations are cancelled before the invoice due date, and where that rule is published.
3. Whether I may re-register or have the registration reinstated, and under what conditions.
