### I. The Green







Today I stood inside **Pelayanan Satu Atap — Polda Metro Jaya**.







I came dressed in **green**.







And Allah SWT describes the inhabitants of Paradise:






> *عَـٰلِيَهُمْ ثِيَابُ سُندُسٍ خُضْرٌۭ وَإِسْتَبْرَقٌۭ ۖ وَحُلُّوٓا۟ أَسَاوِرَ مِن فِضَّةٍۢ وَسَقَىٰهُمْ رَبُّهُمْ شَرَابًۭا طَهُورًا ٢١*







The virtuous will be ˹dressed˺ in garments of fine green silk and rich brocade, and adorned with bracelets of silver, and their Lord will give them a purifying drink. (Surah Al-Insan, verse 21)







I wear green as a daily declaration — a reminder of the destination, not the difficulty of the road. When they try to drag you into the mud, you dress for Jannah.

### II. What Happened — And Why I Am Here







Let me be honest with you, as I have always tried to be.







After my first report was submitted to **Bareskrim Polri**, I expected the harassment to stop. Instead, it escalated.







Those who have targeted me for **2.5 years** across multiple platforms — Instagram, LinkedIn, and beyond — chose not to retreat. They chose to fight back. But not with evidence. Not with facts. Not with legal standing.







They fought back with **public opinion warfare**.







They **doxxed me**. They **doxxed my family**. They surfaced a video of me from 2.5 years ago — a moment of personal vulnerability — and weaponized it, broadcasting it to audiences with the intent to humiliate, discredit, and silence me.







No solid proof. No legal basis. Just noise — carefully engineered noise — designed to make the crowd turn against me before the law could protect me.







But Allah SWT already spoke about this dynamic:







يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ كُونُواْ قَوَّـٰمِينَ لِلَّهِ شُهَدَآءَ بِٱلۡقِسۡطِۖ وَلَا يَجۡرِمَنَّكُمۡ شَنَـَٔانُ قَوۡمٍ عَلَىٰٓ أَلَّا تَعۡدِلُواْۚ ٱعۡدِلُواْ هُوَ أَقۡرَبُ لِلتَّقۡوَىٰۖ وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ خَبِيرُۢ بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ







O you who have believed, be persistently standing firm for Allah, witnesses in justice, and do not let the hatred of a people prevent you from being just. Be just; that is nearer to righteousness. And fear Allah; indeed, Allah is Acquainted with what you do. (Q.S Al-Maidah : 8)

### III. The Stranger — Ghuroba







There is a narration from our Prophet Muhammad ﷺ that has lived in my chest for years:






> حَدَّثَنَا سُفْيَانُ بْنُ وَكِيعٍ، حَدَّثَنَا حَفْصُ بْنُ غِيَاثٍ، عَنِ الأَعْمَشِ، عَنْ أَبِي إِسْحَاقَ، عَنْ أَبِي الأَحْوَصِ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، قَالَ قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ـ صلى الله عليه وسلم ـ " إِنَّ الإِسْلاَمَ بَدَأَ غَرِيبًا وَسَيَعُودُ غَرِيبًا فَطُوبَى لِلْغُرَبَاءِ " . قَالَ قِيلَ وَمَنِ الْغُرَبَاءُ قَالَ النُّزَّاعُ مِنَ الْقَبَائِلِ .







It was narrated from ‘Abdullah that the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) said: “Islam began as something strange and will go back to being strange, so glad tidings to the strangers.” It was said: “Who are the strangers?’ He said: “Strangers who have left their families and tribes.”







Sunan Ibn Majah 3988







The Ghuroba are not outcasts. They are not failures. They are the ones who hold firm to what is right when the world around them has bent, compromised, and gone quiet. They are the ones who file reports when it would be easier to stay silent. They are the ones who dress for Paradise when the crowd is dressing for approval.







When you are doxxed, mocked, and dragged through digital mud — and you still choose the path of integrity — you begin to understand what it means to be a stranger in this world.







Glad tidings to the strangers.







I count myself among them — not with arrogance, but with gratefulness. Because to be strange in a world that rewards cruelty and silence is, I believe, to be exactly where Allah wants you.

### IV. The Reformer — Mujaddid







The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:






> *حَدَّثَنَا سُلَيْمَانُ بْنُ دَاوُدَ الْمَهْرِيُّ، أَخْبَرَنَا ابْنُ وَهْبٍ، أَخْبَرَنِي سَعِيدُ بْنُ أَبِي أَيُّوبَ، عَنْ شَرَاحِيلَ بْنِ يَزِيدَ الْمَعَافِرِيِّ، عَنْ أَبِي عَلْقَمَةَ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، فِيمَا أَعْلَمُ عَنْ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ " إِنَّ اللَّهَ يَبْعَثُ لِهَذِهِ الأُمَّةِ عَلَى رَأْسِ كُلِّ مِائَةِ سَنَةٍ مَنْ يُجَدِّدُ لَهَا دِينَهَا " . قَالَ أَبُو دَاوُدَ رَوَاهُ عَبْدُ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنُ شُرَيْحٍ الإِسْكَنْدَرَانِيُّ لَمْ يَجُزْ بِهِ شَرَاحِيلَ .*







*Narrated Abu Hurayrah:*







*The Prophet (ﷺ) said: Allah will raise for this community at the end of every hundred years the one who will renovate its religion for it.*







*Abu Dawud said: 'Abd al-Rahman bin Shuriah al-Iskandarani has also transmitted this tradition, but he did not exceed Shrahil.*







*Sunan Abi Dawud 4291*







It has now been **100 years** since the fall of the Ottoman Caliphate in 1924.







One century without a unifying structure for the Muslim ummah. One century of fragmentation, colonization of the mind, and the slow erosion of Islamic civilization as a leading force in human progress. One century — and the timer has reset.







I am not declaring myself the Mujaddid. That is for Allah and history to decide. But I am declaring that I feel the **weight of this moment** — and I refuse to look away from it.







The renewal this era needs is not through conquest. It is through **knowledge**. Through **technology**. Through **akhlaq**. Through rebuilding the infrastructure of human dignity — education, healthcare, legal protection, and economic access — for every person who has been left behind by systems that were never designed to include them.







That is the mission of **Infraloka**. That is why I build. That is why, even when harassed, even when doxxed, even when my family's information is thrown into public forums by people with no legal basis and no moral standing — I do not stop.







The work is too important. The moment is too significant. The call is too clear.

### V. Rahmatan Lil Alamin







My name, **Rahmat**, means mercy. And it is not coincidence — it is calling.







Allah SWT said to His Prophet ﷺ:







وَمَآ أَرْسَلْنَـٰكَ إِلَّا رَحْمَةًۭ لِّلْعَـٰلَمِينَ ١٠٧







We have sent you only as a mercy for the whole world.






> *"And We have not sent you except as a mercy to all the worlds."* — QS. Al-Anbiya: 107







**Rahmatan lil alamin** — a mercy for all creation.







Not just for Muslims. Not just for Indonesians. Not just for the educated, the wealthy, the connected. For *all*. For the street vendor outside Masjid Salman. For the farmer in Sulawesi with no legal recourse. For the young graduate in Papua with no access to capital. For every person the system forgot.







This is the north star of everything I do. And no amount of harassment, doxxing, or public opinion manipulation can extinguish it — because it was never mine to begin with. It belongs to the mission. And the mission belongs to Allah.

### VI. Arjuna's Bow — A Lesson from the Mahabharata







In the great epic of the **Mahabharata**, the warrior **Arjuna** stood on the battlefield of Kurukshetra and dropped his bow.







He looked across the field and saw his enemies — and they were his cousins, his teachers, his kin. He was overwhelmed. He said, *"I cannot fight them."*







And Krishna said:







***अशोच्यानन्वशोचस्त्वं प्रज्ञावादांश्च भाषसे।गतासूनगतासूंश्च नानुशोचन्ति पण्डिताः॥***






> *"You grieve for those who should not be grieved for, yet you speak words of wisdom. The wise grieve neither for the living nor for the dead."* — Bhagavad Gita, 2:11







I understand Arjuna's moment deeply now. The hardest battles are not against strangers. They are against people you once trusted — people who were once part of your journey, who now stand across the field, weaponizing what they know about you.







But **dharma** — righteous duty — must be fulfilled. Not out of vengeance. Not out of pride. But because truth, if abandoned by those who know it, leaves the world darker for everyone.







I picked up the bow. I walked into Polda Metro Jaya. And I filed my report.







“Now I am become Death, the Destroyer of Worlds”. These words, from the Sanskrit scriptural text the Bhagavad Gita, are famously attributed to J Robert Oppenheimer, the architect of the atom bomb.







![Article content](/img/arsip-eace98195a05667b8b7696b8.webp)






In Christopher Nolan’s grand biopic, [Oppenheimer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oppenheimer_(film)), the physicist recites these lines not during [the Trinity blast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_(nuclear_test)) (the first detonation of this nuclear weapon) but in a scene with his lover Jean Tatlock.







Oppenheimer later referenced another verse from the Bhagavad Gita when recalling his state of mind as he witnessed the Trinity explosion in the New Mexico desert on 16 July, 1945:






> *If the radiance of a thousand suns were to burst at once into the sky, that would be like the splendour of the mighty one.*







These verses refer to the sublime form, “Vishwarupa”, Lord Krishna takes in the Bhagavad Gita when he reveals his divine nature to the warrior prince, Arjuna.







“Who are you?” asks Arjuna.







“I am Time,” replies Krishna, “powerful destroyer of worlds, grown immense here to annihilate these men”.







Arjuna is blinded by Krishna’s radiance even as he quakes with fear at God’s capacity to destroy evil with the fire emanating from his ferocious visage.







### VII. The Reckoning — A Note on Death Note







For those who believe that by destroying someone's reputation in the court of public opinion they escape accountability, I offer a simple reflection from **Death Note** — the story of a man who believed he could become God through the power of judgment:







***"This World is Rotten and Those Who Are Making it Rot Deserve to Die."***







***"I am justice! I protect the innocent and those who fear evil. I'm the one that will become the god of a new world that everyone desires!"***







Death Note, Episode 1 - "Rebirth"







Every action leaves a trace. Every injustice has its reckoning — in this dunya, or in the akhirah. The digital trail of harassment, doxxing, and coordinated reputation attacks does not disappear. It is documented. It is timestamped. It is evidence.







And unlike the Death Note, the law does not require supernatural power. It only requires patience, documentation, and the courage to stand in the right lobby.

### VIII. A Book for Those Who Helped Me







In the Islamic tradition, gratitude is not merely a feeling — it is an act.







![Article content](/img/arsip-6113ae5de8d889a6fe7465c2.webp)











To everyone who stood beside me during these 2.5 years — those who believed me when it was easier not to, who offered a kind word when the noise was loudest, who checked in quietly without asking for anything in return — **I give you a book**.







Not a certificate. Not a social media shoutout. A **book** — because knowledge is the most enduring gift one human being can give another. It was the first word revealed to our Prophet ﷺ:







ٱقْرَأْ بِٱسْمِ رَبِّكَ ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَ ١ خَلَقَ ٱلْإِنسَـٰنَ مِنْ عَلَقٍ ٢ ٱقْرَأْ وَرَبُّكَ ٱلْأَكْرَمُ ٣ ٱلَّذِى عَلَّمَ بِٱلْقَلَمِ ٤ عَلَّمَ ٱلْإِنسَـٰنَ مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ ٥ كَلَّآ إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَـٰنَ لَيَطْغَىٰٓ ٦ أَن رَّءَاهُ ٱسْتَغْنَىٰٓ ٧ إِنَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ ٱلرُّجْعَىٰٓ ٨ أَرَءَيْتَ ٱلَّذِى يَنْهَىٰ ٩ عَبْدًا إِذَا صَلَّىٰٓ ١٠ أَرَءَيْتَ إِن كَانَ عَلَى ٱلْهُدَىٰٓ ١١ أَوْ أَمَرَ بِٱلتَّقْوَىٰٓ ١٢ أَرَءَيْتَ إِن كَذَّبَ وَتَوَلَّىٰٓ ١٣ أَلَمْ يَعْلَم بِأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَرَىٰ ١٤ كَلَّا لَئِن لَّمْ يَنتَهِ لَنَسْفَعًۢا بِٱلنَّاصِيَةِ ١٥ نَاصِيَةٍۢ كَـٰذِبَةٍ خَاطِئَةٍۢ ١٦ فَلْيَدْعُ نَادِيَهُۥ ١٧ سَنَدْعُ ٱلزَّبَانِيَةَ ١٨ كَلَّا لَا تُطِعْهُ وَٱسْجُدْ وَٱقْتَرِب ۩ ١٩

If you are reading this and you have been harassed, doxxed, or dragged — and you stayed quiet because you were afraid of what they would do next — I want you to know:







**Your silence is not weakness. But your voice, when you are ready, is not weakness either.**
