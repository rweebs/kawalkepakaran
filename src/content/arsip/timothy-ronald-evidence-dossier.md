## Assessment

The comparative analysis rated this case **Extreme** on its Dunning-Kruger scale and called it the most severe of the cases it examined. Its reasoning is the size of the gap between a 2.1-million-follower audience that follows him for teaching about finance, and the recorded academic status and website performance.
