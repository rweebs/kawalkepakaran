## Snapshot

Raymond Chin is the founder of Genesis. His LinkedIn headline reads "Venture Partner & Contrarian Thinker". His Instagram audience was recorded at 1.3 million followers on June 21, 2026. This dossier expands his section of the [Engineering Expertise Comparative Analysis](/arsip/artikel/engineering-expertise-comparative-analysis-infraloka-vs-indonesian-techbro-david-alfa-sunarna-raymond-chin-jerome-polin-timothy-ronald-sabda-ps), using only the evidence recorded there.

## Verified record

**Education.** Universitas Bina Nusantara (BINUS), Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, 2012–2016. He graduated with **Magna Cum Laude** distinction (Certificate of Excellence). The comparative analysis treats this as a legitimate computer science credential.

**Professional experience and domain.** Genesis is a venture builder and investment firm rather than an engineering execution house. The comparative analysis therefore reads the CS degree as a branding asset and not as a reflection of active engineering practice.
