*The speaker at a Telkom-branded event on 8 November 2026 promotes large numbers and well-known partners. This article lists each claim, what would be needed to check it, and what I found.*

This is a record of public claims, not an accusation. Nothing here shows that any claim is false.

## Scope and identity note

- **Subject:** Marchel Andrian Shevchenko, "Founder of Data Sorcerers" on the event poster.
- **Sources:** screenshots I took of his public LinkedIn and of the Data Sorcerers website around 29 August 2026. They are not forensically authenticated, and pages change.
- Credential claims are covered separately in [claims vs. public records](/arsip/artikel/telkom-ai-center-indigo-speaker-claims-vs-public-records-what-is-and-is-not-shown), and in the earlier [AEGIS assessment](/arsip/artikel/aegis-investigative-report-and-credential-assessment-study-case-of-marchel-shevchenko).

## What I found, and what I did not

- I found the claims above on the public pages. I did not find an audit, a published attendance record, or an independent source that confirms them.
- That is the absence of what I found, not evidence of absence. These numbers could be accurate and simply unpublished.
- A logo on a "partners" page can mean many things, from a signed agreement to an event attended by a staff member. Only the institution can say which.
- I searched the national company registry by name for "Data Sorcerers Indonesia" and saw "not found". That search was by name only, from a phone, after a captcha error. It is indicative at most. A formal request to the registry would settle whether a company exists and under what name.

## Why I list claims at all

An event organiser who prints "Founder of Data Sorcerers" next to a corporate logo is relying on these numbers and partners, even if the poster does not repeat them. A short table of claims and checks is cheap to produce and easy to answer.

## Where I may be wrong

- I may have missed a public audit or press confirmation.
- Figures like "users" and "students reached" are defined differently by different organisations. A number can be honest and still not comparable.
- A partner page may be accurate to the speaker's own understanding of the relationship.
