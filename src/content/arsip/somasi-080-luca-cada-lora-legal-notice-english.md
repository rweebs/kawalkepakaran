## My position

The letter states that I never threatened him. The only message of mine that he showed asks for a lawful arrangement and says I would send a somasi otherwise. A somasi is a lawful written warning, and saying I would send one contains no threat of violence, no threat to expose anything and no demand for money or goods. He showed it as a cutout, so I asked for the full conversation. I also asked him to name dates and evidence for the "employer tagged" and "refused to buy a domain" statements.
